Life insurance policies differ in what they pay, when they pay it, and whether part of the premium is saved or invested. These are the main kinds IRDAI describes for policyholders.

## Term insurance

Cover for a fixed period. If the person insured dies during the term (or becomes totally and permanently disabled, where the policy covers that), the nominees are paid the sum assured. Usually nothing is paid if the person outlives the term, which is why term cover costs the least for a given sum assured.

## Endowment policy

Insurance combined with saving, with a set maturity date. The sum assured is paid if the person insured dies during the term; if they survive it, the maturity amount is paid. Money-back plans are a variant that pays part of the sum assured at intervals during the term and the rest at maturity, while usually keeping cover for the full sum assured.

## Whole life insurance

Cover for the rest of the person's life rather than a fixed term, so a death benefit is paid whenever death occurs while the policy is in force. It is often used to leave money to heirs.

## Unit linked insurance plan (ULIP)

Insurance combined with investment. After charges, the rest of the premium buys units in funds the policyholder chooses, and switching between funds is usually allowed. The value moves with the funds' performance, and the policyholder carries the investment risk. On death, the policy pays the sum assured to the extent covered. Before buying, check the total charges, the lock-in period, surrender charges and surrender value.

## Annuity (pension) plans

Turn a sum of money into a regular income, usually for retirement.

- Immediate annuity: a single lump-sum payment, with income starting straight away.
- Deferred annuity: regular payments (or one payment) build up a fund until a chosen vesting date, after which the fund buys the income. The policy terms set how much, if any, can be taken as a lump sum at vesting.

## Choosing between them

- Term insurance is the plain way to protect dependants; the other kinds mix protection with saving or investing, at a higher premium for the same cover.
- Any of these kinds can be offered with unit-linked investment.
- Read the policy's terms, charges and exclusions before buying. See also "How much life insurance cover you need".
