IRDAI lists the factors that decide how much life insurance a person needs: the number of dependants, the lifestyle to provide for them, children's education, investment needs, and what the person can afford. Two common ways turn these into an amount.

## The needs method

Add up what the family would need if the earner died, then subtract what is already available.

A worked example:

- Outstanding home loan: ₹20,00,000
- Children's education goal: ₹15,00,000
- Five years of family expenses at ₹40,000 a month: 5 × ₹4,80,000 = ₹24,00,000
- Total needs: ₹59,00,000
- Less savings and existing cover: ₹9,00,000
- Cover needed: ₹50,00,000

## The income multiple

A quicker rule of thumb sets cover at a multiple of yearly income, often 10 to 15 times. It ignores loans, goals and savings, so it can be far from the needs figure for a particular family.

## Points to keep in mind

- The needs change: a new loan or a child raises them; repaid loans and growing savings lower them. Reviewing the figure every few years keeps it current.
- Inflation raises future expenses, so long-dated needs are usually raised for it.
- Life cover replaces income; health insurance, which pays medical bills, is a separate need with its own sum insured.
- The premium has to be affordable for the whole term for the cover to stay in force.

The figures are for illustration only.
