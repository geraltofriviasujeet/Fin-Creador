These figures are from the First Schedule (Part I-B) and the Memorandum to the Finance Bill 2026, for tax year 2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027). The old regime applies only to someone who opts out of the default section 202 rates (see "Old or new tax regime: how the choice works").

## The rates

Each rate applies only to the part of income inside its band.

Under 60:

- Up to ₹2,50,000: nil
- ₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000: 5%
- ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000: 20%
- Above ₹10,00,000: 30%

Resident aged 60 to 79:

- Up to ₹3,00,000: nil
- ₹3,00,001 to ₹5,00,000: 5%
- ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000: 20%
- Above ₹10,00,000: 30%

Resident aged 80 or more:

- Up to ₹5,00,000: nil
- ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000: 20%
- Above ₹10,00,000: 30%

## Rebate, surcharge and cess

- Rebate (section 156): a resident individual whose total income is up to ₹5,00,000 gets a rebate of the tax, up to ₹12,500.
- Surcharge, a percentage of the tax: 10% above ₹50 lakh up to ₹1 crore; 15% above ₹1 crore up to ₹2 crore; 25% above ₹2 crore up to ₹5 crore; 37% above ₹5 crore. On dividends and on capital gains taxed under sections 196 to 198, surcharge is capped at 15%.
- Marginal relief: just above each surcharge threshold, the tax plus surcharge cannot exceed the tax plus surcharge at the threshold by more than the income above it.
- Health and education cess: 4% of the tax plus surcharge.

## Worked examples (cess included)

Under 60, taxable income ₹5,00,000. Tax: 5% of ₹2,50,000 = ₹12,500, fully cancelled by the rebate: nil.

Under 60, taxable income ₹9,25,000. Tax: 5% of ₹2,50,000 (₹12,500) + 20% of ₹4,25,000 (₹85,000) = ₹97,500. The income is above ₹5,00,000, so there is no rebate. Cess ₹3,900; total ₹1,01,400.

Taxable income ₹7,00,000 at different ages:

- Aged 60 to 79: 5% of ₹2,00,000 (₹10,000) + 20% of ₹2,00,000 (₹40,000) = ₹50,000. Cess ₹2,000; total ₹52,000.
- Aged 80 or more: 20% of ₹2,00,000 = ₹40,000. Cess ₹1,600; total ₹41,600.

Under 60, taxable income ₹51,00,000. Tax: ₹1,12,500 + 30% of ₹41,00,000 (₹12,30,000) = ₹13,42,500. Surcharge at 10% would be ₹1,34,250. At ₹50,00,000 the tax is ₹13,12,500 with no surcharge, and the income above it is ₹1,00,000, so tax plus surcharge is capped at ₹14,12,500: marginal relief cuts the surcharge to ₹70,000. Cess ₹56,500; total ₹14,69,000.

## What the old regime keeps

The standard deduction on salary is ₹50,000 (section 19), and deductions such as sections 123, 124(3) and 126 count (see "Deduction limits under sections 123, 124 and 126").
