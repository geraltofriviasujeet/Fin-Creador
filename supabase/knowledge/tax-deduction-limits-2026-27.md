These figures are from the Income-tax Act, 2025, as published on the Income Tax Department's website, for tax year 2026-27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027). Sections 123, 124 and 126 replace the 1961 Act's sections 80C, 80CCD and 80D. Apart from the employer's pension contribution, these deductions are available only to someone who opts out of the default section 202 rates (the old regime).

## Section 123: up to ₹1,50,000

Individuals and Hindu undivided families may deduct what they paid or deposited in the tax year in the items listed in Schedule XV, up to ₹1,50,000 in total. The Schedule includes, with conditions, life insurance premiums, an employee's provident fund contributions and notified savings schemes such as PPF.

## Section 124: pension scheme

- An individual's own contribution to a notified pension scheme (such as NPS): up to ₹50,000 (section 124(3)). Payments into a minor child's account by a parent or guardian share the same ₹50,000 limit (section 124(4)).
- The employer's contribution: up to 10% of salary, or 14% for a Central or State Government employer (section 124(1)). Under the default section 202 rates, every employer's limit is 14% (section 124(2)).

## Section 126: health insurance and medical costs

- Health insurance for yourself, your spouse and dependent children: up to ₹25,000; ₹50,000 if the person insured is a senior citizen.
- Health insurance for parents: a separate ₹25,000; ₹50,000 if a parent is a senior citizen.
- Preventive health check-ups count within these limits, up to ₹5,000, and may be paid in cash. Everything else must be paid by a mode other than cash.
- Medical spending on a senior citizen who has no health insurance: up to ₹50,000.
- Each group (self and family; parents) is capped at ₹50,000 in all.

## A worked example (old regime)

- PPF ₹1,00,000 and EPF ₹72,000: ₹1,72,000, capped at ₹1,50,000 under section 123.
- Own NPS contribution ₹50,000 under section 124(3).
- Health insurance ₹22,000 for the family and ₹38,000 for senior-citizen parents: ₹60,000 under section 126.
- Total deductions: ₹2,60,000.
