# Knowledge base: links to publishers

Every `source_url` and `read_more_url` in `supabase/knowledge/` is a link to a publisher's
site, so each publisher's hyperlinking policy applies (checked 2026-11-13):

| Publisher | Linking policy | What we link |
|---|---|---|
| RBI | Home page after notifying RBI in writing; other pages need prior permission | Home page only, until permission for deep links |
| SEBI | Direct links allowed without prior permission; asks to be told | Investor site pages |
| AMFI | Home page only | Home page |
| IRDAI (policyholder.gov.in) | Direct links allowed without prior permission | Pages |
| EPFO | Direct links allowed without prior permission; asks to be told | Home page |
| Income Tax Department (incometaxindia.gov.in, incometax.gov.in), indiabudget.gov.in | Prior permission for any link | Nothing yet |

Subject: Notice of a link to the Reserve Bank of India home page

>
> In line with the hyperlinking terms in the disclaimer on www.rbi.org.in, I am writing to
> notify the Reserve Bank of India that FinCreador, a personal finance application now in a
> private test with invited users, links to the RBI home page (https://www.rbi.org.in/).
>
> The link appears beside short explainers written in our own words on general topics such as
> budgeting, emergency funds, inflation, loan EMIs, prepayment, credit card interest and
> credit reports. Each explainer is labelled "FinCreador explainer, based on RBI" and the link
> is shown as "Read more on RBI's website". We do not reproduce RBI content, use RBI's name or
> emblem to suggest endorsement, or load RBI pages in a frame; the link opens in a new window.
>
> We would also like to request permission to link directly to specific pages of RBI's

> the list of pages and the exact link text for your review.
