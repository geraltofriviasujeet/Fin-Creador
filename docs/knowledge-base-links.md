# Knowledge base: links to publishers

Every `source_url` and `read_more_url` in `supabase/knowledge/` is a link to a publisher's
site, so each publisher's hyperlinking policy applies (checked 2026-11-13):

| Publisher | Linking policy | What we link |
|---|---|---|
| RBI | Home page after notifying RBI in writing; other pages need prior permission | Home page only, until permission for deep links |
| SEBI | Direct links allowed without prior permission; asks to be told | Investor site pages |
| AMFI | Home page only | Home page |
| IRDAI (policyholder.gov.in, irdai.gov.in) | Direct links allowed without prior permission | Pages |
| EPFO | Direct links allowed without prior permission; asks to be told | Home page |
| Income Tax Department (incometaxindia.gov.in, incometax.gov.in), indiabudget.gov.in | Prior permission for any link | Nothing: tax explainers name the department with `link_permission_pending: true` until permission is given |

## Notification to RBI (owner sends)

To: the RBI contact listed on the website's "Contact Us" page

Subject: Notice of a link to the Reserve Bank of India home page

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> In line with the hyperlinking terms in the disclaimer on www.rbi.org.in, I am writing to
> notify the Reserve Bank of India that FinCreador, a personal finance application now in a
> private test with invited users, links to the RBI home page (https://www.rbi.org.in/).
>
> The link appears beside short explainers written in our own words on general topics such as
> budgeting, emergency funds, inflation, loan EMIs, prepayment, credit card interest and
> credit reports. Each explainer is labelled "FinCreador explainer, based on RBI" and the link
> is shown as "Read more on RBI's website". We do not reproduce RBI content, use RBI's name or
> emblem to suggest endorsement, or load RBI pages in a frame; the link opens in a new window.
>
> We would also like to request permission to link directly to specific pages of RBI's
> financial education material, so readers reach the relevant page. We would be glad to share
> the list of pages and the exact link text for your review.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> <your name>
> FinCreador
> <your contact email>

## Permission request to the Income Tax Department (owner sends)

The incometaxindia.gov.in hyperlinking policy asks for a request stating the nature of the
content on the linking pages and the exact link text.

To: the Web Information Manager listed on www.incometaxindia.gov.in

Subject: Request for permission to link to the Income Tax Department's website

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> In line with the Department's hyperlinking policy, I request permission for FinCreador, a
> personal finance application now in a private test with invited users, to link to pages on
> www.incometaxindia.gov.in.
>
> Nature of the content: short explainers written in our own words for tax year 2026-27
> under the Income-tax Act, 2025, on the default tax rates under section 202, the deduction
> limits under sections 123, 124 and 126, how the choice of regime works, and capital gains
> basics. Each explainer is labelled "FinCreador explainer, based on Income Tax Department
> material" and states the tax year it covers. We do not reproduce the Department's pages, use
> its name or emblem to suggest endorsement, or load its pages in a frame; links open in a new
> window.
>
> Pages we would link to: the Department's pages for sections 2, 19, 123, 124, 126, 156,
> 196, 197, 198 and 202 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
>
> Exact link text: "Read more on the Income Tax Department's website".
>
> We would be glad to provide anything else you need.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> <your name>
> FinCreador
> <your contact email>
